Research

My research lies at the intersection of cognitive neuroscience and artificial intelligence.

Since September 2023, I have been working part-time on a PhD under the supervision of Frédéric Alexandre, head of the INRIA Mnemosyne team. We take inspiration from natural cognition to endow artificial agents with metacognitive abilities.

The PhD defense is scheduled for Spring 2027.
