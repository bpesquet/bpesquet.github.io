My research lies at the intersection of cognitive neuroscience and artificial intelligence.

Since September 2023, I have been working part-time on a PhD under the supervision of [Frédéric Alexandre](https://www.labri.fr/perso/falexand/), head of the INRIA [Mnemosyne](https://team.inria.fr/mnemosyne/) team. We take inspiration from natural cognition to endow artificial agents with metacognitive abilities.

The PhD defense is scheduled for Spring 2027.
